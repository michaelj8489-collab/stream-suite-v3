What happens next?

We are going to ask you a few simple questions to build your broadcast. Here is the plan:

  • 1. Devices: Pick your camera and microphone.
  • 2. The Waiting Room: Pick the picture and music people experience before you start.
  • 3. The Main Stage: Type your name and pick the colors for when you are talking.
  • 4. The DJ Booth: Show us the folder where your MP3 music files are saved.
  • 5. Screen Share: Pick a window if you want to show your computer screen.
  • 6. The Keys: Paste your secret passwords so we can broadcast your show to the world!

Step 1: Pick your devices

Let's make sure Stream Suite can see your camera and microphone. Select them from the dropdowns below.

Step 2: The Waiting Room

This is what your audience will see while they wait for you to go live. Set your background, timer text, and pre-show music.

Step 3: The Main Stage

This is your main camera view. Enter your name, choose your overlay theme, and set up your background audio.

Background Audio (Auto-Ducks to 1%)

Step 4: The DJ Booth

This is the scene for playing music. Set up the automated folder where Stream Suite will read your tracks.

⚠️ CRITICAL: How to Name Your MP3s

For the graphics engine to display the correct song info on screen, your files MUST be named exactly like this:

01_Song Title_Artist Name.mp3

Step 5: Screen Share

If you plan to show a website, presentation, or specific window during the show, select it here.

Step 6: The Keys

The final step! Paste your stream keys, audio server info, and chat URLs. When you click Launch, Stream Suite will open your tools.
